Even though she dines out with collegues or clients for the most of time, Jenny still manages to cook her own meal from time to time. She usually would cook light meals 4 times a week, and at most 1-2 times mashed potatos. It would be nice for her to own a potato masher, but it’s not a necessity for her.

Design Features

Here are some key features from the final design.

Here are some more renderings of the product.

Double-sided mashing head for a little surprise. Choice of silicone material, is scratch-proof for the pot, bowl or any kitchen utensils.

Sharp tip for poking into the potatoes to check if they’re fully cooked. With a pop of bright pink in the mouth area, it also has brought the whole design some brightness and vividity.

Rubber-made pattern for better friction. It took inspiration from real flamingo feathers, which is also one of a highlight of the design.
