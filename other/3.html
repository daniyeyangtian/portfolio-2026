Insights

Opportunities

• Parental Occupation: With limited time after work, parents seek tools that make evenings and weekends more engaging and meaningful for family bonding.

​

• Balanced Education: Parents value a holistic educational approach, which aligns with products that support shared learning and creativity.

​

• Evening Routines: Families spend evenings at home, creating a need for interactive tools to enhance bonding moments.

​

• Family Activities: Shared storytelling, games, and creative tasks are key bonding activities, emphasizing the value of tools that integrate fun and connection.

​

• Children’s Age: The majority of children fall between 3-8 years old, where emotional connection and family activities are crucial for development.

Design Features

To leave other surfaces clean, coherent and aesthetically pleasing, the on/off button is placed at the bottom, along with the charging inout.

​

Instead of a full circle, the 3/4 circular

charging input give the users a clearer

guided direction about the angles and positions they need to put in the charging nest.

The metal mesh is chrome aluminum alloy in warm grey, in contrast with the matte and soft body. The color is in consistent with the holistic color choice---pleasing, warm and approaching.

​

While, the gentle glow of the mesh has brought the product tender. Just as its shape, a perfect teardrop from any angle, is minor but major.

The vivid and symbolic eyes inspired from the real birds have brought life to the product, in a joyful and relaxed way to improve user experience.

​

With the key element added, the speaker itself has become more adaptable for children, without being too childish for the parents.

Design Semantics

“Beyond Charging...”

The charging nest is made of ABS, where the outer case has rough texture, and the inner charging plate is smoothly matte. Beside the contrast in texture, the outer case is light brown, and the inner plate is chocolate brown.

The birds, nest is their home when they are little. For humankind, home is where possibly all the sweet, warm and unforgettable memories lie in. Itʼs the place where we could take a break, and feel reassured. Itʼs the place for charging.
