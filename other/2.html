Overview

A ratchet wrench and related accessory system that helps determine torque with the ease of expertise, proposing a fastener system designed to help non-professional users correctly remove and install fasteners in emergency situations while providing torque guidance during reassembly, which ensures that fasteners are tightened to a safe torque level, preventing failures caused by loosening.

Fastener-related issues are common during assembly and pose significant safety risks. Ensuring that fasteners are properly installed is therefore critical. EASYTORQUE proposes a fastener system designed to help non-professional users correctly remove and install fasteners in emergency situations while providing torque guidance during reassembly. The system ensures that fasteners are tightened to a safe torque level, preventing failures caused by loosening.
